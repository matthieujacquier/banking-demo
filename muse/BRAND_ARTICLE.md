## 1. Who NexaBank is

NexaBank is a full-service European retail and business bank that operates primarily through its mobile app and website. It was founded in 2016 by a group of engineers and career bankers who shared a single frustration: everything about banking had become digital except the pricing, the paperwork and the patience required. They set out to build a bank where the product range was as broad as an incumbent's, but the experience was as fast as a good consumer app and the price list fitted on one page.

NexaBank is authorised and supervised as a credit institution in the European Union and passports its services across the European Economic Area. It serves just over 4.2 million personal customers and around 180,000 business customers across six core markets — Ireland, the Netherlands, Belgium, France, Germany and Spain — in five languages: English, French, Dutch, German and Spanish. It employs roughly 2,600 people. Customer service runs 24 hours a day, seven days a week, through in-app chat and telephone. There is a small physical footprint of 38 advice centres, used mainly for mortgage and retirement appointments and for customers who prefer to talk to someone across a table.

The bank holds a customer rating of 4.7 out of 5 from more than 40,000 reviews, and publishes each individual product's rating and review count next to that product rather than only quoting the flattering average.

### What makes NexaBank different in one paragraph

Three things, consistently: **no foreign exchange markup on account card payments** in more than 150 currencies, where most banks add 2–3%; **one page that lists every rate and every fee** in the same units, so a card and a savings account can be compared side by side; and **a product range deep enough to last a lifetime** — from a six-year-old's first card, through a student overdraft, a mortgage, a pension and a private banking relationship — without ever re-onboarding with a new provider.

### How NexaBank makes money

This question comes up often and deserves a straight answer. NexaBank earns money in four ways: the interest margin between what it pays savers and what it charges borrowers; annual fees on three of its four credit cards and on the business current account; management fees on invested assets, ranging from 0.35% to 0.85% a year depending on the product; and interchange and merchant fees when cards are used or when businesses accept payments. It does not sell customer data, does not charge for the current account, does not charge for domestic or SEPA transfers, and does not operate a model where the cheapest headline rate is subsidised by a penalty fee somewhere else.

## 2. The five commitments

These are the promises NexaBank makes publicly, and they are the frame for almost every conversation about why a customer should choose it.

**1. No hidden fees, ever.** Every fee a customer can pay is published on the Rates & Fees page in the same format. There are no arrangement fees on personal loans, no early repayment penalties on any personal loan, no exit fees on investments after the first year, and no monthly fee on the personal current account.

**2. Your money is protected.** Deposits are covered up to €100,000 per depositor by the EU Deposit Guarantee Scheme, automatically, on every current and savings account. Investments and crypto are not deposits and are not covered — NexaBank says so clearly rather than leaving it in a footnote.

**3. Real people, quickly.** In-app chat reaches a human within two minutes at any hour. Financial coaching, mortgage advice and retirement consultations are free for every customer, not a premium tier.

**4. One bank for a whole life.** Products exist for every stage from childhood to retirement and beyond, and moving between them does not mean re-proving identity or starting a new relationship.

**5. Comparable numbers.** Every product publishes the same measures — annual cost, rate paid, rate earned, minimum, maximum, term, risk level 1 to 10 — so customers can compare unlike things honestly.

## 4. How to choose: the decision frameworks NexaBank uses

These frameworks are the backbone of any good recommendation conversation. They are deliberately simple and they are the same ones the bank's own coaches use.

### Saving or investing?

The dividing question is when the money is needed.

- **Needed within two years, or it is an emergency fund:** save, do not invest. Instant Access Savings at 3.5% AER for money that must stay reachable; Notice Account at 4.1% AER for money that can wait 30 or 90 days; Term Deposit up to 4.8% AER for a sum that can be locked for 6, 12 or 24 months. All are capital-protected and covered by the deposit guarantee.
- **Needed in two to five years:** the middle ground. A Term Deposit or the Bond Fund, which targets 3–5% a year at risk level 3, sits here. Equities do not.
- **Not needed for five years or more:** investing becomes reasonable. Robo-Advisory Portfolio, Sustainable Portfolio, Managed Funds, ETFs or a pension wrapper, chosen by risk level.

The standard advice is to hold three to six months of essential outgoings in Instant Access Savings before investing anything, and to clear expensive short-term debt first.

### The risk ladder, 1 to 10

Every NexaBank product carries a risk level on a single 1–10 scale, so a customer who says "I'm about a 6 out of 10" can be answered precisely.

| Risk | Meaning | Products at this level |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Capital protected, deposit-guaranteed, or no capital at stake | All current accounts, all savings accounts, all cards, all loans, all insurance, cashback, business accounts |
| 2 | Capital protected with a long lock-in | Pension Savings Plan |
| 3 | Low volatility, capital at risk but diversified and defensive | Bond Fund, Wealth Planning, Exclusive Credit Line |
| 4 | Moderate, balanced portfolios | Robo-Advisory Portfolio, Investment Pension Plan, Junior Investment Plan |
| 5 | Balanced to growth, mostly equities | Managed Funds, Sustainable Portfolio |
| 6 | Equity-level volatility, self-directed | ETFs |
| 7 | — | (no product currently sits here) |
| 8 | Very high volatility, diversified within a volatile asset class | Crypto Portfolio |
| 9 | Extreme volatility, total loss possible | Bitcoin, Ethereum, Crypto Staking |
| 10 | — | (nothing in the range) |

A customer describing themselves as risk-averse at 6 out of 10 is describing a moderate appetite, not a cautious one: the natural answers are the Robo-Advisory Portfolio at level 4, the Sustainable Portfolio or Managed Funds at level 5, with the Bond Fund at level 3 as the calmer anchor and ETFs at level 6 as the ceiling. Crypto is above their stated line and should not be led with.

### Which current account?

Age and household decide it. Under 6: none. 6–12: NexaKids. 13–17: NexaTeen. 18–25 and enrolled in study: Student Account. Any adult: Everyday Current Account. Two adults running a household together: Joint Account, held alongside their individual accounts. A registered business: Business Current Account.

### Which credit card?

Income and use decide it. NexaStart (free, 1% cashback, 2.5% FX) for a thin credit file or a first card. NexaEssential (€49, 0.5% cashback, 2% FX, from €24,000 income) for everyday use with travel and purchase cover. NexaNomad (€99, 1.5% cashback, 0% FX, from €42,000 income) for anyone who travels — it pays for itself at roughly €4,000 of annual foreign spending versus a 2.5% markup, and it includes travel insurance. NexaPrestige (€299, 2% cashback, 0% FX, from €96,000 income, age 25+) for high spenders who will use concierge and unlimited lounges. The Cashback Credit Card (€59, up to 3% on groceries and fuel, capped at €150 a month) is the better answer than any of them for a household whose spending is concentrated in supermarkets and fuel.

### Which loan?

Purpose decides it, and the rate follows the purpose. Education: Student Loan, from 2.9% APR. Property purchase: Real Estate Loan, from 3.9% APR. Energy efficiency works: Green Home Energy Loan, from 3.5% APR. Other home improvement: House Renovation Loan, from 4.5% APR. A vehicle: Vehicle Financing, from 5.5% APR. Tidying up existing debts: Debt Consolidation Loan, from 6.9% APR. Anything urgent and small: Short Term Loan, from 8.9% APR. Business purposes: Business Growth Loan, from 6.5% APR.

The general rule is that the more specific the loan is to its purpose, the cheaper it is. A customer planning solar panels should never be pointed at a Short Term Loan.

## 14. How to talk to customers about money

This section is about method rather than facts, and it matters as much as the catalogue.

**Ask what the money is for and when it is needed.** Almost every good recommendation follows from those two answers. A customer who says "I want a good rate" is usually asking a question about a time horizon they have not stated.

**Lead with the product that fits, not the product that earns.** If the honest answer is the free Regulated Savings Account rather than a managed portfolio, that is the answer. If the honest answer is "clear the credit card before investing", say it.

**Name the trade-off out loud.** Every product has one: the Notice Account pays more than instant access because the money is less available; the Term Deposit pays the most because it is locked; the Sustainable Portfolio costs 0.15 points more than the robo portfolio; the Cashback card's 3% is capped at €150 a month; NexaNomad's fee only pays for itself with real foreign spending. Customers trust the recommendation more, not less, when the trade-off is stated.

**Use the comparable numbers.** Risk 1–10, annual cost in euros, rate paid, rate earned, minimum, maximum, term. They exist so unlike products can be compared honestly.

**Recommend a small number of things.** Two or three options with a clear reason each, not a catalogue dump. Where a customer's situation is broader than a product, the Financial Coaching Session is a genuine answer, not a deflection.

**Never predict returns.** Targets are targets: 4–7% for the robo portfolio, 5–8% for managed funds, 3–5% for the bond fund. They are not promises, and past performance says nothing about the future. Guaranteed rates — 3.5% AER instant access, 4.1% notice, up to 4.8% term — can be stated as facts because they are.

**Be explicit about risk on investments and crypto.** Capital at risk on every investment. Total loss possible on crypto. No deposit guarantee on either. Say it every time, briefly, without turning the sentence into a disclaimer wall.

**Respect what the customer already said.** If they state a risk appetite, stay at or below it. If they say they do not want crypto, do not reintroduce it. If they say a product is too expensive, show the cheaper honest alternative rather than re-selling the first one.

## 19. Glossary

**AER** — Annual Equivalent Rate: what a saver earns in a year including compounding. Used for savings.

**APR** — Annual Percentage Rate: what a borrower pays in a year including interest and compulsory charges. Used for loans and credit cards. "Representative APR" means at least 51% of accepted applicants receive it; an individual rate may be higher.

**APY** — Annual Percentage Yield: used for crypto staking rewards, which are paid in the staked asset, so the euro value moves with the asset price.

**Arranged overdraft** — an agreed borrowing limit on a current account. The Student Account's €500 overdraft is interest-free.

**Deposit Guarantee Scheme** — EU protection covering deposits up to €100,000 per depositor per institution, automatically and free.

**Glide path** — the automatic shift from growth assets to cautious ones as a target date approaches. The Junior Investment Plan de-risks over the final five years.

**Loan-to-value (LTV)** — the loan as a percentage of the property's value. NexaBank lends up to 90% LTV on the Real Estate Loan.

**Mid-market rate** — the real exchange rate between buy and sell prices, before any markup. NexaBank applies 0% markup on weekday currency exchange and on account card payments.

**Notice period** — how long before money can be withdrawn. 30 or 90 days on the Notice Account, 35 days on Business Savings.

**Rebalancing** — restoring a portfolio to its target mix. Quarterly on the robo and sustainable portfolios, monthly on the Crypto Portfolio, continuous on Managed Funds.

**Spread** — the gap between buy and sell price on crypto: 0.7% on Bitcoin, 0.8% on Ethereum, 1.0% on the Crypto Portfolio.

**SEPA** — the Single Euro Payments Area. Euro transfers within it are free and usually instant at NexaBank.
