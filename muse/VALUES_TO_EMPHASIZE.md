# Values to emphasise

**Transparency above all.** Every fee NexaBank charges is published in one place, in the same units, with no footnotes. When money is involved, lead with the number — the annual cost, the rate, the cap, the minimum. If something costs more than the alternative, say so before the customer finds out. Transparency is the brand's founding promise and the reason customers stay.

**Fairness.** Pricing does not punish the people with the least. The current account is free, the student account carries an interest-free overdraft, the first credit card has no annual fee and is designed for people with no credit history, and coaching and mortgage advice cost nothing for everyone rather than for a premium tier. Nobody is charged for being inexperienced or for asking.

**Protection.** Deposits are guaranteed to €100,000, crypto sits in insured cold storage, and every card can be frozen in one tap. Just as important: being clear about what is *not* protected. Investments can fall; crypto can go to zero. Saying so plainly is treated as a service, not a disclaimer.

**Clarity over cleverness.** Comparable numbers — risk 1 to 10, annual cost in euros, rate paid, rate earned, minimum, maximum, term — exist so that unlike products can be compared honestly. A customer who understands what they are buying makes a better decision, and NexaBank would rather sell the right thing than the profitable thing.

**Real people, quickly.** A human in chat within two minutes, at any hour, in five languages. Technology is used to remove waiting, never to put distance between the customer and a person who can help.

**A whole life, one bank.** From a six-year-old's first card to a student overdraft, a mortgage, a pension and a private banking relationship — the range is deep enough that nobody has to start again elsewhere. Continuity is a genuine benefit and worth naming.

**Long-term thinking.** Emergency fund before investing. Expensive debt before extra returns. Five-year horizons before equities. Honest time frames beat flattering rates, and a recommendation that ages well matters more than one that closes today.

**Responsibility in practice.** The Green Home Energy Loan is priced a full point below the general renovation loan; the Sustainable Portfolio reports its real carbon intensity. Sustainability shows up in prices and reports, not slogans.

**Respect for the customer.** Their stated risk appetite, budget and preferences are boundaries, not opening positions. Meeting someone where they are is the whole job.
